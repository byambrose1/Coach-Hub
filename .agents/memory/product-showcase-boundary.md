Keep the public product showcase separate from access to the real coach dashboard. Show workflows beyond the dashboard and explain benefits for coaches and their clients without exposing customer data or allowing demo payments.

**Why:** The user clarified that a dashboard link alone does not meet the showcase goal; visitors need to understand the wider product, while the owner separately needs real signed-in billing access.

**How to apply:** Keep clear sign-in access alongside the public tour. Label examples as fictional, and never present a tour as evidence that live subscription billing works.
