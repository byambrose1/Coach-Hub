name: Product audience and client forms
description: Owner wants a straightforward hub for independent coaches, with forms beyond fitness PAR-Q.
---
Do not position Practably solely for fitness trainers or make PAR-Q the only supported form workflow.

**Why:** On 2026-10-03 the owner described independent coaches who want to manage a client roster with ease and without complicated infrastructure, and gave therapists as an example of users needing their own forms.

**How to apply:** Describe the calendar, sessions, client forms, invoices and payment workflows in plain language. Support coach-created custom forms alongside optional PAR-Q screening. Do not imply medical suitability or clinical certification.

Custom forms must support both client completion through private links and coach-entered responses within client records.

**Why:** On 2026-10-03 the owner explicitly selected both workflows.

**How to apply:** Preserve both when extending forms. Private links remove the need for a client account, but do not prove the submitter's identity or constitute a verified signature. Do not market them as signed consent or identity verification.

Assigned forms must preserve the questions originally shared, even when a reusable template is edited or removed.

**Why:** Later template changes must not silently change what a client was asked or invalidate an in-progress response.

**How to apply:** Apply template changes to new assignments. Changing an already shared questionnaire requires an explicit new request; deleting a template must not delete historical responses.

Basic custom forms must remain included on Free.

**Why:** On 2026-10-05 the owner explicitly said, “the free plan should allow basic forms,” while requesting secure document uploads to private forms.

**How to apply:** Keep both private-link completion and coach-entered basic forms available without upgrading. Do not infer a new paid restriction merely from adding document uploads.
