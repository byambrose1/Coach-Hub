---
name: Subscription refund policy
description: Owner-approved scope and safety expectations for the voluntary 24-hour refund guarantee.
---
Offer automatic full refunds of the first subscription payment when requested within 24 hours of successful payment. Cancel the subscription immediately, return the coach to Free, and retain their client data. Renewals and repeat subscriptions do not receive a fresh guarantee.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose automatic first-payment refunds after discussing customers who quickly realize the service is not needed. They were informed that Stripe retains original processing fees.

**How to apply:** Keep cancellation, the refund request, and bank settlement distinct in customer-facing language. Preserve eligibility for finishing an already accepted request after a partial failure, even if the original window has since expired. Additional payments, existing unrelated refunds, and ambiguous payment histories require manual review rather than risking an incorrect refund. The guarantee supplements statutory rights.
