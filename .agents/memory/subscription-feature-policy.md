name: Subscription feature policy
description: Owner's scope rules for tier restrictions, privacy and invoice branding.
---

The owner chose genuine feature restrictions, not client limits alone, and explicitly corrected the inclusion of privacy controls as a paid-plan benefit. Keep privacy protections available across plans.

**Why:** Privacy is not a premium feature of this product.

**How to apply:** Do not add privacy controls to paid pricing benefits or restrict them during future subscription changes.

The owner requested the Practably logo on Free-plan invoices. This request did not specify a paid tier for branding removal.

**Why:** The explicit requirement was Free invoice branding, not a new paid white-label promise.

**How to apply:** Preserve the coach's identity as invoice issuer, include the actual Practably mark on Free invoices, and clarify any future advertised white-label entitlement rather than inferring its tier.
